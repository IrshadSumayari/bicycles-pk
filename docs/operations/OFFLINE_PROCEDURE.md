# Internet outage — انٹرنیٹ بند

**Keep this page beside the numbered, carbon-copy paper sales book.**

## Stop and check — پہلے جانچیں

When the till says **“Offline — use the paper sales book”**, stop using Pay.
If it says a sale could not be confirmed, call a manager to check **Sales / Transactions**
before taking payment again. The first payment may already have been recorded.
Do not start another sale or charge the card twice to get past an error.

## Write every sale — کاغذی رسید

Use the next numbered slip and give the customer the carbon copy. Record:

- Date, time, slip number and cashier initials.
- Each item, SKU/barcode if available, size, colour, quantity or fabric meters.
- Unit price, discount and total; customer name/phone if needed.
- Payment method, amount received and cash change. For cards, note the terminal
  slip reference and last four digits only. Never write the full card number.

Take **cash**, or use the **standalone card machine** and keep its approved slip.
Check the terminal approved the payment. Do not promise store credit or take digital
payments that you cannot verify. Keep the cash with the till and both slips together.

## Returns and deliveries — واپسی اور حوالگی

Pause returns and tailoring deliveries. A manager may approve an exception only
when the paper slip clearly shows the outstanding balance and payment history.
Write any refund, balance paid or garment handed over on the numbered slip.

## Back online — دوبارہ آن لائن

Wait for green. A manager first checks for any uncertain earlier POS payment.
Then enter each unrecorded paper sale **once, in slip-number order**, using the POS
note **“Paper #123”** (replace 123 with the actual slip number). Use the original
payment method and reconcile the amount with the paper slip. **Do not run an already
approved card payment through the terminal again.** Enter cash sales before counting
and closing the shift, so the recorded cash and stock match the physical till.

Mark each slip with its POS invoice number. Check totals, stock and cash, then sign
and file the book with the card slips. Keep all copies; do not tear out failed slips.
